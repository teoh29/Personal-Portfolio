About
The story of
Teoh En Yi

Born and raised in the cozy town of Ipoh, Perak, I’m now diving deep into the world of code as a Year 1, Semester 3 Computer Science student at Sunway University. Growing up in a warm and supportive family taught me the magic of kindness—and perhaps where my friendly, chatty nature comes from too! Whether it’s debugging a stubborn error or exploring new frameworks, I truly enjoy the process of learning, experimenting, and solving problems. I’m naturally curious, always eager to grow, and I believe every line of code brings me one step closer to building something meaningful.

Looking ahead, I aspire to become a skilled software developer with a strong focus on building user-centric applications that make everyday tasks easier and more enjoyable. I’m especially interested in front-end development and UI/UX design, where creativity meets functionality. My goal is to contribute to innovative tech projects that have a real impact on people’s lives, while continuously learning new technologies and refining my craft along the way. Whether it’s in a startup environment or a collaborative tech team, I’m excited about the journey ahead and the challenges that will shape me into the developer I aim to become.
